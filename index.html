Type in the Latin alphabet. Each letter is carved as the matching Britannian rune. The pairs th, ee, ng, ea, and st each have a rune of their own, as on the alphabet chart used from the later games.

Alphabet

Click a rune to insert it. In the Ultima IV alphabet, published without Q, V is written with the same rune as U.

Letterforms follow the runic alphabet chart on the Ultima Codex. An apostrophe keeps a pair apart and is not carved: moon'glow leaves n and g as two runes.
